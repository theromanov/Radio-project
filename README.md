# 🎙 Online Radio Intensive — Landing Page

A promotional landing page for an online intensive course about launching and growing your own radio station.  
The project is designed to attract users through a strong value proposition, structured content blocks, and clear calls to action.

---

## 🚀 Overview

This landing page presents a two-day free intensive that teaches users how to:

- Launch their own online radio station
- Optimize and promote it effectively
- Drive traffic and grow audience reach
- Generate revenue from their station

The layout focuses on conversion-driven design with a bold hero section, informative content blocks, and a strong CTA strategy.

---

## 🧩 Page Structure

### 1. Hero Section

- Clear headline with strong hook: _"Create Your Own Radio Station — It’s Simple!"_
- Supporting subtitle emphasizing expertise and real challenges
- Primary CTA button: **Sign Up for Free**
- Background image related to radio/audio production

### 2. What Awaits You in the Intensive

- Overview of two full training webinars
- Visual icon-based blocks explaining:
  - How to launch your radio station
  - Website optimization for search engines
  - Traffic growth and analytics basics

### 3. Why Online Radio and Why It Is Needed

- Explanation of market relevance
- Benefits of online broadcasting
- Opportunities for independent creators
- Emphasis on accessibility and global reach

### 4. Outcomes After Completion

- Key results users will achieve:
  - Launch their own station
  - Proper technical setup
  - Traffic growth understanding
  - Audience development
  - Monetization opportunities
